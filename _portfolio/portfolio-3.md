This was my final project for COMM 2206 at BCIT. The goal was to create customized materials for a specific environment and audience. Given my prior experience with ADP Workforce Now, I thought it apt to create a quick start guide catered towards employees in retail who: 

* Don't have a lot of time or interest for long, complex documents
* Have loose sheets lying around everywhere in the break room
* Come from various technological backgrounds

The document was created using Microsoft Word. The full piece may be viewed [here](/files/COMM2206_Part3_AidanLee.pdf)
